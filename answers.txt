Q1: Why might we consider using Git?

Answer: It is connected to github and allows for multiple people to collaborate on a project together. 
It also has a checkpoint system so that you can revert back to a previous working state if you mess up the current one.

Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: It can be used in the finance field where you can look at a dataset of all factors that influence GDP and see witch ones are 
contributing the most to a higher GDP.

Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: Merging joins two branches together and creates a new "merge commit" that ties their histories and Rebase moves the feature 
branch commits on top of the latest tip of another branch
